Hover states

Nine directions on the real type, at the real sizes, in the real colours. Hover each one. Tab through them too — the focus ring is the same on all six.

Each is a single class with no per-link markup, so any of them can be dropped onto a and survive CMS text of any length.

This folder is not part of the site. Delete demo/ before handoff.

1b Painted swipe — black smear, true magenta reclaims #FF00FF

The comp's link colour is pure #FF00FF. It measures 2.76:1 on the off-white ground and fails AA, which is why the live site runs a darkened #C100C1. On the ink strip, measured off the rendered swatch, it is 6.05:1 and passes. The restraint on the resting state stops being a compromise and becomes a setup for this. The strip is the yellow one's own torn silhouette repainted near-black, so the ragged edge is the same hand as the rest of the collage.

2 Torn-paper underline works in prose

The hairline is replaced by a slice of a real torn strip. At rest it is a thin ragged rule — still a non-colour signal, which links inside prose need. On hover it thickens and rises, like the strip sliding up from under the baseline. Pale pink was the first choice and vanished at hairline weight — the one signal that must not be quiet. Blue carries.

5 The painted word use once

Not the ransom-note image swap I talked you out of. Your traced Black Paint webfont is the painted alphabet, so the word just changes face — no image per glyph, the text stays selectable, find-in-page still works. The painted copy is layered over the Garamond one and cross-faded, so the line never reflows. An easter egg on the zine's name, not a link system.

6 Travelling squiggle works in prose

A seamlessly-tiling SVG wave shifted by exactly one wavelength on a loop, so it travels forever. At rest it is the underline — still a non-colour signal. On hover it starts moving and the colour deepens. The path is uneven on purpose: second and third harmonics at odd phases give crests of differing height, so it still tiles perfectly but reads as drawn rather than computed. Six corrections to the stock recipe are documented in the CSS, the largest being that the usual viewBox='0 0 20 4' clips its own troughs flat.

7a Travelling dotted rule works in prose no SVG

The squiggle's mechanism without its one real wart. A data-URI SVG can't see currentColor, so the squiggle bakes its colour in and carries a copy per state; radial-gradient is native CSS and inherits the link colour for free. No SVG, no encoding traps, six lines your dev can read at a glance. Pitch and dot size are in em, so the rule scales with the type — ~5.9px pitch under the 27px prose, ~4.2px under the 19px phone size.
